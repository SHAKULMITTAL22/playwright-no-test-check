## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/home
2. Click 'GET STARTED NOW'
3. Click 'Ok'
4. Click 'TRANSFERS'
5. Click 'MAKE A TRANSFER'
6. Select 'SINGLE' frequency
7. Select 'CHECKING' as 'Transfer From'
8. Select 'SAVING' as 'Transfer To'
9. Enter amount
10. Click 'Save'
