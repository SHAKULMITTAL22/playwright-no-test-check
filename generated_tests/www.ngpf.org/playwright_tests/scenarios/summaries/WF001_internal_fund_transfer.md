## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/transfer
2. Click 'GET STARTED NOW'
3. Click 'TRANSFERS' in the sidebar
4. Click 'MAKE A TRANSFER'
5. Select 'SINGLE' frequency
6. Select 'CHECKING' as source and 'SAVING' as destination
7. Enter amount and date
8. Click 'Save'
