## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/pay-bill
2. Click 'BILLS' in the sidebar
3. Click 'MANAGE RECIPIENT'
4. Click 'ADD RECIPIENT', enter name, and click 'SUBMIT'
5. Click 'PAY BILL'
6. Select 'SINGLE' frequency, select the new recipient, enter amount, and click 'SUBMIT'.
