## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/pay-bill
2. Click 'GET STARTED NOW'
3. Click 'BILLS' in the sidebar
4. Click 'MANAGE RECIPIENT'
5. Click 'ADD RECIPIENT', enter name, and click 'SUBMIT'
6. Click 'PAY BILL' in the sidebar
7. Select 'SINGLE' frequency, select the new recipient, enter amount and date
8. Click 'SUBMIT'
