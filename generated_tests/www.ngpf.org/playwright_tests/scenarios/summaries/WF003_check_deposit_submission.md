## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/deposit-check
2. Select destination account from 'To' dropdown
3. Enter amount
4. Click 'Front' to upload front image
5. Click 'Back' to upload back image
6. Click 'Submit'.
