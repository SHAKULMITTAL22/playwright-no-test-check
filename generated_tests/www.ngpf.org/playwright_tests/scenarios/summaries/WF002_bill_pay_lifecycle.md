## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/pay-bill/manage-recipient
2. Click 'ADD RECIPIENT', fill in details, and click 'SUBMIT'
3. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim/pay-bill
4. Select 'SINGLE' frequency, select the new recipient, enter amount, and click 'SUBMIT'
5. Navigate to 'DISPLAY ALL BILLS' and click the trash icon to delete the bill.
