## Navigation Hint

1. Navigate to https://www.ngpf.org/bank-sim
2. Click 'GET STARTED NOW'
3. Click 'Ok' to dismiss modal
4. Expand sidebar 'TRANSFERS' and click 'DISPLAY ALL TRANSFERS'
5. Confirm Past Transfers table reveals completed/scheduled transfers, each with Id, Date, Description, Amount
6. Validate transfer record appears matching submitted values from previous scenario
